# Project Report: E-Commerce Platform Analysis

## Executive Summary

This report analyzes the performance and growth metrics of our e-commerce platform for Q4 2024. 
The platform recorded a 34% increase in monthly active users and a 28% improvement in conversion rates.

## Key Findings

### Traffic & Engagement
- Monthly Active Users: 1.2M (+34% YoY)
- Average Session Duration: 4m 32s (+12%)
- Bounce Rate: 38% (-5 percentage points)
- Pages per Session: 6.2 (+0.8)

### Revenue Metrics
- Gross Merchandise Value: ₹48.6 Crore (+41%)
- Net Revenue: ₹7.2 Crore (+38%)
- Average Order Value: ₹1,240 (+8%)
- Customer Acquisition Cost: ₹320 (-15%)

### Top Product Categories
1. Electronics – 32% of GMV
2. Fashion & Apparel – 24% of GMV
3. Home & Decor – 18% of GMV
4. Beauty & Personal Care – 14% of GMV
5. Books & Stationery – 12% of GMV

## Technical Performance

The platform maintained 99.94% uptime during Q4. Key infrastructure improvements included:
- Migration to microservices architecture (completed October 2024)
- Redis caching layer reducing DB load by 60%
- CDN integration cutting page load times from 2.8s to 0.9s

## Recommendations

1. **Mobile App**: 68% of traffic is mobile — prioritize native app development
2. **Personalization**: Implement ML-based product recommendations
3. **Logistics**: Partner with 3 additional last-mile delivery providers for Tier-2 cities
4. **Payments**: Add UPI Autopay and BNPL options to reduce checkout friction

## Conclusion

Q4 2024 demonstrated strong growth fundamentals. With continued investment in mobile experience 
and personalization, we project Q1 2025 GMV to reach ₹55–60 Crore.
